

Design brief:
- First-layer units compute compact signed features:
  - Units 0-3: linear error signals for the paddle position versus the predicted bottom-row ball column under the four diagonal regimes (down-right, down-left, up-right, up-left).
  - Units 4-5: signs of horizontal and vertical ball movement from ball minus trail.
  - Units 6-11: wall-hit helpers: ball/trail at left/right edges and dynamic error signals for the bottom target after a descending wall bounce.
- Second-layer units are high-threshold AND gates that fire only when a movement direction and the sign of the corresponding error match.
- Output layer maps those gates to high logits for left or right; no-op is the default fallback.
